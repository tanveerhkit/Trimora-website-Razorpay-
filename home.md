# Trimora

Book trusted salon services with ease.

Trimora is a salon discovery and appointment booking platform that helps customers find salons, browse services, view availability, and book appointments online. We work with salon partners to make booking simple, transparent, and reliable.

## What You Can Do on Trimora

- Discover salons and grooming services
- View service details, prices, and timings
- Book appointments online
- Manage bookings, cancellations, and support
- Connect with salons through the Trimora platform

## How Trimora Works

1. Browse salons and services available on Trimora.
2. Select your preferred date, time, and service.
3. Make payment, where applicable, through secure online payment methods.
4. Visit the selected salon at the booked time and enjoy the service.

## Important Note

Trimora is a technology platform that facilitates discovery, booking, and payment for salon services. The actual salon services are provided by independent salon partners.
